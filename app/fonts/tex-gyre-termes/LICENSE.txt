TeX Gyre Termes 2.004, by B. Jackowski and J. M. Nowacki (GUST e-foundry),
from CTAN: https://mirrors.ctan.org/fonts/tex-gyre/opentype/
Based on URW++ Nimbus Roman No9 L.

Modified for RecipePrinter: texgyretermes-regular.otf and texgyretermes-bold.otf
were cut down to the Latin characters a recipe card uses and converted to WOFF2
(fontTools pyftsubset), under new file names as the GUST Font License requests.
No outlines or metrics were changed.
