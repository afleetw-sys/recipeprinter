# RecipePrinter voice

How the app, the site and every error message talk.

- **Talk like a cook.** Concrete kitchen nouns, short sentences.
- **Errors: what happened, then what to do.** "We couldn't finish your cookbook PDF. Try again in a moment."
- **Say what someone still has before what they could buy.**
- **No exclamation marks, emoji or em dashes.** Buttons stay plain.
- **Say "free" only where it's true.**
