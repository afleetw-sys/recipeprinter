Brown Butter Banana Bread

Posted in Baking · 118 Comments · Jump to Recipe

The first time I made this, it was raining, and I had four bananas going soft on the counter and absolutely no intention of doing anything productive with my afternoon. That is, I think, the correct emotional starting point for banana bread.

My grandmother made banana bread every Sunday, in the same scratched loaf pan she'd had since the sixties. She never measured anything. I have spent an embarrassing number of weekends trying to reverse-engineer a loaf I last ate when I was eleven, and I want to walk you through every wrong turn I took, because I think the wrong turns are the interesting part.

Advertisement

The first six attempts were too sweet. The next four were too dense. Somewhere around loaf number twelve I started browning the butter, and that was the thing. Browning the butter takes eight minutes and it is the entire difference between a fine banana bread and one people ask you about later.

Why brown butter?

When butter heats past the point where its water cooks off, the milk solids toast and throw off a nutty, almost caramel smell. You are not adding an ingredient so much as unlocking one you already had. It is the highest return on eight minutes available in a home kitchen.

A few notes on the pan. I use a light-colored metal loaf pan. Glass runs hotter at the edges and you will get a dark crust before the center sets. Dark nonstick does the same thing faster.

Choosing your bananas

You want them past the point where you would eat one. Fully spotted, going black at the stem, soft enough that the peel tears rather than splits. If yours are stubbornly yellow, roast them in the skin at 300°F for fifteen minutes and let them cool.

A note on flour

All-purpose is correct here. Bread flour makes it chewy in a way that reads as a mistake. Cake flour makes it fall apart when you slice it warm, which you will, because you have no self-control and neither do I.

Storage

Wrapped on the counter it holds three days. In the fridge it goes stale faster, which surprises people. It freezes beautifully: slice it first, then you can pull out one piece at a time.

Brown Butter Banana Bread

Prep 15 min · Bake 55 min · Serves 8

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe bananas, mashed
  • 1/2 cup brown butter, cooled
  • 3/4 cup brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 3/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp fine salt

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F and butter a loaf pan.
  2. Brown the butter in a light pan until the solids turn golden and it smells nutty, about 8 minutes. Cool slightly.
  3. Whisk the bananas, butter, sugar, and eggs until smooth.
  4. Fold in the flour, baking soda, and salt just until no dry streaks remain.
  5. Pour into the pan and bake 55 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean.
